Launch the `idea-generator` subagent (foreground) to brainstorm improvement ideas for Meridian, per its instructions. If the user passed arguments ($ARGUMENTS), use that to focus the brainstorm on a specific area (e.g. "calendar", "onboarding", "analytics"); otherwise let it range across the whole app. Relay its ideas back to the user in full — this is a menu for them to react to, not something to summarize away.
